This task is an extension of the basic problem Tensor I. Our recommendation is to implement the basic variant first, then extend it to match Tensor II interface.

The problem to solve is the same, however, the following has changed:

the dimension of the tensor may be 1, 2, or 3,
the dimension of the view may be 1, 2, or 3.
Notes:
Normally, this extra functionality would be an optional test in a single taks. However, a separate task was chosen (technical reasons).
Submit your solution to both Tensor I and Tensor II, the overall assessment is the sum.
